ABI findings:
- Every naked entry begins PUSHFD/PUSHAD, stores the raw frame in EBX, aligns
  ESP down to 16 bytes, reserves 12 bytes and pushes the cdecl frame argument.
  The instruction immediately before CALL therefore has 16-byte-aligned ESP.
- The compiler generated no entry prologue before PUSHFD. Every return/resume
  restores ESP from EBX, then POPAD/POPFD. Caller cleanup remains in native code.
- Raw saved_esp is native ESP minus 4. The compiled pixel/glyph helpers load
  their first native stack argument at saved_esp+8. Line loads x2/y2/color at
  saved_esp+8/+12/+16, preserving the original ECX/EDX coordinate convention.
- Read results are written to raw-frame EAX at +1C, and fixed inline reads to
  raw-frame EDI at +00. Nonvolatile GPR saves/restores match the cdecl contract.
- F68D5 resumes only after MOV [native ESP+18],EAX, after POPFD, preserving the
  original prior TEST flags. 11454F reissues TEST BYTE [ESI],2 after POPFD.
